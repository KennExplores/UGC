The person behind the lens

About Me

Kenn Blackman — Canberra, Australia

I'm Kenn, a UGC creator based in Canberra, Australia, and a gamer who lifts heavy things. I play World of Warcraft, and I make content the way I actually live: at the gym, at my desk and out in the world. I film and edit everything myself, so the gaming references land and the gym footage looks like gym footage, not an ad. As a former Twitch and YouTube Partner, I know how short-form video is watched: a fast hook, a clear idea, text you can read in a second and an ending that makes people react. I work with gaming, gym and lifestyle brands, whether that's a quick product demo, a themed series or ongoing editing from your raw footage.
