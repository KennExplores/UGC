About Me

Kenn Blackman — Canberra, Australia

I'm Kenn — a UGC and content creator based in Canberra, Australia. I create content the way I actually live — at the gym, out on the trail, at a solo campsite, or working through a morning routine. That means whatever you send me gets used properly, in context, by someone who genuinely cares about the outcome. I work with fitness, outdoor, grooming, and lifestyle brands and bring the same attention to detail to every project, whether it's a quick product demo or a longer-form piece of storytelling.
